# Vendored: battlefield-editor

This directory vendors the MIT-licensed **battlefield-editor** cinematic battle
engine by 林亞澤 (Yaze Lin) — https://github.com/yazelin/battlefield-editor

Used under the MIT License (see ./LICENSE). Its Three.js renderer is embedded to
play cinematic replays of battles produced by this project's own live battle
engine (see src/web/battle/bfe*). Large media assets (audio/narration/glTF) from
the upstream example packages are NOT vendored.
